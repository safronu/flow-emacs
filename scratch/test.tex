\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{amsmath, amssymb}
\begin{document}

Type \verb|mm| then TAB, or \verb|dm| then TAB, to expand math snippets.

Place point inside a math expression and press \textbf{F5} to preview the
formula inline as an SVG overlay.  \textbf{F6} previews the whole buffer,
\textbf{F7} clears all overlays.

Test the golden path — put point in $ e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0 $ and hit F5.

Longer display formula (F5 while inside it):
\begin{equation}
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
\end{equation}

Matrix (put point inside, F5):
\[
  A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix},
  \quad \det A = ad - bc
\]

\end{document}
