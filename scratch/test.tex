Type \verb|mm| then TAB, or \verb|dm| then TAB, to expand math snippets.

Place point inside a math expression and press \verb|C-c p p| (Android
Emacs) to preview the formula inline as a PNG overlay.
\verb|C-c p b| previews the whole buffer, \verb|C-c p c| clears all
overlays.

In Termux Emacs the equivalent keys are \textbf{F5} (preview at point),
\textbf{F6} (preview whole buffer), and \textbf{F7} (re-open the last
render in Android's image viewer — this does \emph{not} clear anything;
Termux Emacs has no in-buffer overlays to clear).

Test the golden path — put point in $ e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0 $ and hit
\verb|C-c p p| (or F5 in Termux Emacs).

$e^{i*\pi}$

Longer display formula (\verb|C-c p p| / F5 while inside it):
\begin{equation}
  \int _{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
\end{equation}

Matrix (put point inside, \verb|C-c p p| / F5):
\[
  A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix},
  \quad \det A = ad - bc
\]
